How it works

Three steps. That's all.

Set up in less time than it takes to find last year's question paper.

i.

Sign up with your college email

Verified students only. Your campus, your people.

ii.

Choose your courses

Notes, papers and deadlines for everything you're enrolled in, gathered automatically.

iii.

Study with less chaos

Share, upvote, find a study group, and walk into exams prepared.

Questions

Frequently asked

If yours isn't here, write to us.

Is NerdStack free?

Yes. The core features are free for students, always. Notes and previous-year papers will stay free.

When are you launching?

Soon. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first to get access.

Which colleges are supported?

We're starting with a small number of campuses and expanding from there. Ask your friends to join the waitlist to move your campus up the list.

Can I contribute or join the team?

Yes. We're students building for students. If you write code, design, or simply have ideas, get in touch.
